# ShopFès — Shipping Policy

Version: 2026-09
Document type: policy (fictional company, created for the MLOps mini-project)

## Delivery zones

ShopFès delivers to all cities in Morocco. International shipping is not available.

## Delivery times

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business
days and is available in Fès, Casablanca, Rabat, Tangier and Marrakech only.

## Shipping fees

Standard delivery costs 30 MAD and is free for orders above 500 MAD.
Express delivery costs 60 MAD for all orders.

## Order tracking

A tracking number is sent by email as soon as the order leaves the warehouse.
You can also follow your parcel from Order History in your ShopFès account.
